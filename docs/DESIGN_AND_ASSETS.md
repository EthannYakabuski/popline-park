# Design and asset review

Popline Park chooses a three-lane, automatically moving skate line: steer, ollie, land, repeat. Campaign rides are roughly 22–34 seconds before pauses. Rails reward timing; dodging obstacles keeps the starting gear viable. Gear improves comfort and earned currency without gating campaign completion.

The review covered all **44 available Kenney packs**, recorded in `ASSET_REVIEW.json`. Mini Skate provided the strongest cohesive unused scene kit: animated skaters, board, rails, ramps, bowls and platforms. Nature, City Commercial and UI supply the surrounding park, buildings, controls and real rating stars. Interface and Impact Sounds supply feedback. The mellow music loop is original procedural audio. Illustrated branding and the achievement atlas were generated for this project; they are marketing illustrations, not runtime screenshots.

Two concepts were compared before selection: a compact skate-line arcade game and a cave extraction/mining loop. The cave kit was strong but the latter invited more inventory/crafting menus and overlapped existing resource-loop projects. The skating concept puts its central action on screen immediately and makes fuller use of Mini Skate.

Modest market review, 11 November 2026:

- [Touchgrind Skate 2](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=se.illusionlabs.skate2) demonstrates demand for skate controls with depth. Popline chooses simpler lane/tap input rather than a physics simulation.
- [Skate City: New York press kit](https://skatecitygame.com/newyork/presskit/) emphasizes accessible skating and repeatable challenges. Short routes and an endless mode fit that broad usage pattern.
- [Pocket Skate press kit](https://www.playpocketskate.com/press-kit.html) is a direct reference for compact arcade sessions.
- [Orecraft](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.P3G.OreCraft) illustrates the larger crafting/economy surface of the alternative mining direction.

These are qualitative comparisons, not sales forecasts or evidence that this game will acquire players. A preliminary name search did not surface a relevant exact-name result; no trademark clearance is claimed.

Read-only visual benchmarks: InfiniteSlash, PiratePlunder, Open Road Ramble and The Final Outlaw. No benchmark app identity, production service mappings, signing material or player saves were copied. Shared adapter patterns and supplied plugin code retain their licenses.

District palettes: Palm Promenade (lilac and turquoise), Brickyard Groove (warm clay and sunset), Blue Hour Pier (cool blue and mauve). The route kit remains consistent for readability. Future concept only, not shipped: Night Market Sessions with lantern plaza routes and evening cosmetics.
